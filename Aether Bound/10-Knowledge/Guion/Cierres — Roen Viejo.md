# Cierres — Roen Viejo (Bookend, variable por final)

> Pago de la promesa de la apertura ("I'm going to tell you what happened
> on mine"). Mismo escenario — taberna, mismo Roen — variable según el
> final que el jugador vivió. **Formato conversación, no monólogo**
> (decisión de Boris, 2026-08-07): alguien en la taberna le pregunta,
> Roen contesta corto — la pregunta hace el trabajo que antes hacía la
> narración corrida. **Duración objetivo ~1:30 por cierre** (ajuste
> 2026-08-07 — la v1 en conversación quedó en ~45 segundos, corta a la
> mitad): el tiempo extra sale de que **el Barkeep habla más**, no de
> que Roen se explique de más — su economía sigue siendo rasgo de
> personaje. **Ninguno nombra al Pivote ni menciona detalle
> institucional exclusivo de una sola ruta** (Juramento de Forja, quién
> pagó a quién, etc.) — sigue el mismo patrón que ya usa
> [[Roen-Ficha-Expandida-v1]] §"Roen en cada final", que tampoco nombra a
> ningún Pivote. Diálogo en inglés (regla 9); resto del archivo en
> español.

## Puesta en escena común

Misma taberna que la apertura, mismo Roen. Cada cierre retoma
exactamente donde la apertura cortó — sin repetir el bloque de
worldbuilding.

**BARKEEP** — sin nombre propio, personaje fijo de las 5 variantes (no
uno nuevo por final). Lo conoce hace años; sabe que Roen viene, se
sienta, pide lo mismo, y a veces habla si lo dejan preguntar. No es
invasivo — es persistente, con la confianza de quien ya se ganó el
derecho a insistir un poco. Nunca pregunta por nombres — pregunta por lo
que pasó, no por quién. Coherente con que Roen "nunca busca al jugador":
acá es al revés — a Roen lo buscan a él, y responde corto, nunca de más.

## F1 — The Guided Molt

**Ancla canónica** ([[Roen-Ficha-Expandida-v1]] §F1): *"He estado donde
estás. El arrepentimiento es el precio. Espero que valga."* + "no
consuela, organiza."

```
The Barkeep sets a drink down without being asked. Doesn't leave right
away.

BARKEEP
You never did finish. Told me you'd get to it, that was —
what, three winters ago now?

ROEN
Didn't think you were keeping count.

BARKEEP
I keep count of everything in this room. Occupational hazard.
So. What happened, the night everything went dark?

ROEN
We didn't stop her. Didn't try, in the end.

BARKEEP
That simple?

ROEN
Lights went out. Not just ours — three kingdoms' worth, near
enough. Took years to learn how to live without what we'd
built on top of her.

He turns the ring. Doesn't look up.

BARKEEP
Years is a long time to relearn how to live.

ROEN
Wasn't just me relearning. Whole street. Whole city, further
out than that.

BARKEEP
And you? What'd you end up doing, all that relearning?

ROEN
I run a house now. Feed whoever needs feeding. Wasn't the
ending I had in mind.

BARKEEP
No offense, but you don't strike me as a man who feeds
people for the joy of it.

ROEN
(almost a laugh, not quite)
No. Started because somebody had to. Kept going because I
was good at it.

BARKEEP
(quieter, almost not a question)
Any regrets?

ROEN
I've been where you're sitting, wondering that. Regret's the
price. I'm still paying it.

(beat)

Still think it was worth it.

The Barkeep doesn't push further. Wipes down the bar, moves on.

CUT TO BLACK.
```

## F2a — The Long Winter: Handed Over

**Ancla canónica:** *"La gente que arriesga todo para defender lo que
ama... merece ser recordada. Incluso cuando compra tiempo en vez de
justicia."* — traducida y sostenida, sin ablandarla.

```
BARKEEP
So she made it. That's what you said, wasn't it. Made it.

ROEN
She's alive. Somewhere behind a wall I never got close to
again. That's the whole of it. She's alive, and we bought
time with her. Not justice.

BARKEEP
There's a difference?

ROEN
Ask anyone who's ever had to choose between them.

He doesn't turn the ring this time. Just holds it still.

BARKEEP
You don't sound like a man who thinks he made the right call.

ROEN
Didn't say that either.

BARKEEP
Sounds like you're still deciding if it was a fair trade.

ROEN
People who risk everything for what they love deserve to be
remembered. Even when what they buy you is time. Not
justice.

BARKEEP
That an answer?

ROEN
(a half-shrug)
Best one I've got, this many years on.

BARKEEP
You ever hear anything? About her, after?

ROEN
I don't know what became of the time we bought. Nobody tells
you that part.

He picks up his drink for the first time. Doesn't finish it.

CUT TO BLACK.
```

## F2b — The Long Winter: Fallen

**Ancla canónica:** *"No hay una forma de decir esto bien. Dejé de
buscarla."* — este cierre es el pago directo del beat sembrado en su
propia ficha: no encontró las palabras esa noche. Treinta años después,
las encuentra. **Prohibido cualquier tono de moraleja/"aprendimos algo"**
(regla de F2b, [[Los 5 Finales]] §F2b) — el cierre no puede resolver en
consuelo. **El tiempo extra acá sale del Barkeep insistiendo y siendo
rechazado, no de Roen hablando más** — es el único cierre donde extender
la duración sin romper la regla de "sin moraleja" significa alargar el
silencio, no el contenido.

```
The fire cracks. Roen is quiet longer than in any other version of this
scene. Long enough that the Barkeep almost walks away.

BARKEEP
(carefully)
You don't have to —

ROEN
I sat with what was left of her that night. Tried to say
something. Couldn't find it.

The Barkeep waits. Roen doesn't continue right away.

BARKEEP
(gently)
Take your time.

ROEN
Told myself I'd find it later. Never did. Not for thirty
years.

He sets the ring down on the bar, for the first time — not turning it,
just letting it sit there. The Barkeep glances at it, says nothing about
it.

BARKEEP
Thirty years is a long time to be looking for something.

ROEN
Wasn't looking, most of it. Stopped looking pretty early.
Just — carried the not-having-it.

BARKEEP
(after a pause, careful not to push)
And tonight?

ROEN
There's no good way to say this. I stopped looking for one.

(long beat)

That's it. That's the whole thing. I'm not going to dress it
up thirty years late.

The Barkeep doesn't say anything else. Doesn't offer comfort, doesn't
try to fill it. Just stays close a moment longer than he has to.

He picks the ring back up. Doesn't put it back on.

CUT TO BLACK.
```

## F3 — The Conqueror's Clause

**Ancla canónica:** *"I've buried people I should've protected. I'm not
doing that again."* — dicha, según su ficha, **antes** de irse, no
después. Roen se fue sin ceremonia y no volvió a ver al grupo. El cierre
tiene que sonar distinto a los otros cuatro: **más corto en lo que Roen
sabe, más frío, con menos certeza sobre cómo terminó**, porque Roen
genuinamente no estuvo ahí para el final. Acá el Barkeep empuja más que
en los otros, porque Roen da menos por su cuenta.

```
BARKEEP
You keep saying "that one" different than the rest. Like
it's not finished.

ROEN
Because it isn't. Not for me.

BARKEEP
Explain that.

ROEN
I left before the end of that one. Told them why, once, and
meant it.

BARKEEP
Why?

ROEN
I've buried people I should've protected. Wasn't going to do
it again. So I didn't stay to watch.

BARKEEP
That's not really an answer either. Watch what?

ROEN
(flat, closing the door on it)
Something I didn't want a hand in.

BARKEEP
Fair enough. And after? You never went back, asked around?

ROEN
I don't know how it finished. Not really. Heard pieces, years
later, secondhand. Didn't ask for more than that.

He looks at the fire, not at anything in particular.

BARKEEP
That bother you? Not knowing?

ROEN
Some nights.

BARKEEP
And tonight?

ROEN
Some stories, you don't get the ending. You get the part
where you left.

The Barkeep doesn't ask again.

CUT TO BLACK.
```

## F4 — The Warden's Choice

**Ancla canónica:** *"This is what guarding looks like. Not what you own
— what you honor."* + *"You did it right. That doesn't mean you get to
keep her."* — únicas dos líneas del elenco que ya están en inglés en el
canon original; se citan casi textuales. Vale para ambas variantes
(Pivote vivo o muerto) sin necesidad de bifurcar — la línea nunca habla
del Pivote, solo de Speck y del jugador.

```
BARKEEP
Heard this one has a better ending. That true?

ROEN
Depends what you mean by better.

BARKEEP
Go on, then.

ROEN
She chose. That's the part people forget when they tell it
back to me. She chose. Nobody carried her out of there.

BARKEEP
Better, then.

ROEN
(not quite agreeing)
Better than the alternative. Doesn't make it easy.

BARKEEP
No?

ROEN
I told the one who did it right, that same night. Didn't
feel like praise, saying it. Felt like something closer to
the truth.

He almost smiles. Almost.

BARKEEP
What'd you tell them?

ROEN
This is what guarding looks like, I said. Not what you own.
What you honor.

BARKEEP
And they took that well?

ROEN
You did it right. That doesn't mean you get to keep her.
That's what I told them. Wasn't looking for it to land soft.

BARKEEP
Did it? Land soft?

ROEN
No. Wasn't supposed to.

(beat, softer)

Still true, though. Thirty years on, still true.

The Barkeep nods, like that settles something, and finally moves off to
close up.

CUT TO BLACK.
```

## Test de la regla 2 (fuga de info sobre los otros 8 Pivotes)

- **¿Algún cierre nombra a un Pivote?** No — los 5 usan "the one who did
  it" / "she" / "they" o no mencionan a nadie específico, igual que la
  ficha de Roen. Las preguntas del Barkeep tampoco piden nombres nunca —
  pregunta por hechos, no por identidad.
- **¿Algún cierre menciona detalle institucional exclusivo de una ruta?**
  No — nada de Juramento de Forja (Torgan), pagos del Council (Bram/Iven),
  Royal Academy (Sereth), etc. Todos los cierres hablan solo de Speck, el
  grupo y Roen.
- **¿El cierre de F3 revela quién es el único Pivote para el que F3 es
  imposible?** No — nunca se nombra a Bram ni se da a entender que hay
  una excepción de ruta. Roen simplemente no vuelve a hablar del Pivote
  después de irse. La línea nueva "something I didn't want a hand in" es
  deliberadamente vaga — no dice qué es "eso".
- **¿Algún cierre confirma o descarta el estado vivo/muerto del Pivote en
  F4?** No — la línea de F4 es agnóstica a propósito, ya que la ancla
  canónica original tampoco lo menciona.

**Resultado: pasa el test.** Ninguno de los 5 cierres da información
utilizable sobre qué Pivote traicionó ni sobre las otras 8 rutas.
